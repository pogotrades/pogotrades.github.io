Hello there. If you are here to learn more about the trade value of Pokemon you have or are trying to get, you've come to the right place. There are a few things you should know before you look at the tier list though.

- First, and most importantly, value is subjective. We all have our opinions, and this tier list is just meant to reflect the general consensus among experienced traders. If you feel differently, you do not have to follow my advice!

- If something isn't on here, it's either because the trade value is negligible, or it is too new and hasn't been added yet. I intend to make monthly updates but they may be less frequent if I am busy.

- By the way, this page is designed to be viewed on phones, so it might look weird if you are using a different device.
